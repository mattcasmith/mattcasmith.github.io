About MattCASmith

London, UK  >  Writing on technology, business, and culture from a cyber security professional and ex-journalist – sceptical, but never cynical.
📓 Field Notes is my monthly newsletter full of personal observations.

Thanks for stopping by! I'm Matt, a former journalist working in cyber security. I write a weekly blog about how we build and live with technology, as well as a monthly newsletter.

I try to cut past the hype and hysteria that dominates mainstream tech writing, and am passionate about people and companies who care deeply about what they do and produce quality even when nobody's watching.
